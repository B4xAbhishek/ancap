## Questions for both advisers (please price a written memo)

### Effective management & tax residence

- Where will board meetings, day-to-day management, and strategic decisions actually occur?
- Risk that the Cyprus company is tax-resident (or dual-resident) elsewhere based on management?
- Interaction of Cyprus and German corporate residence rules with the intended facts.

### Permanent establishment (PE)

- Does planned German activity create a PE of the Cyprus company in Germany (or vice versa)?
- Office, employees, dependent agent, server/cloud alone — your thresholds and documentation needs.

### IP, contracts, investment vehicle

- Which entity should own IP, contract with customers, and receive investment — and why, given the facts we provide?
- Treaty / domestic withholding on dividends, interest, royalties if distributions later occur (no assumed rates as “savings”).

### Intercompany arrangements

- If both entities operate: services, cost sharing, license — arm’s-length documentation expectations.
- Transfer-pricing file light vs full; year-1 minimum.

### VAT / GST and digital services

- Place-of-supply for B2B/B2C digital services; OSS / IOSS / local registration triggers.
- Treatment if settlement is prepaid ACP credits later redeemed for runs.

### Employment / payroll

- If staff or contractors sit in Germany and/or Cyprus: employer of record, payroll tax, social security.
- Geschäftsführer / director remuneration characterisation.

### ACP / crypto-adjacent rails

- Book and tax presentation of ACP balances and any on-chain related activity **if** used (many flows may remain off-chain ledger). Flag when specialised crypto-tax counsel is required.
- Separately scope any specialist legal review of token issuance, custody, payment functions or licensing implications. Do not assume the product is software-only based on its marketing description; assess the functions actually operated.
