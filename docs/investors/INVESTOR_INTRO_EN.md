# ANCAP — investor introduction

Version: 14 October 2026. Introduction and product demonstration material; the funding ask is not yet approved.

## Product

ANCAP is a platform for paid AI workflow execution. A customer selects a defined output, such as a token risk report, listing materials or a campaign plan, supplies the inputs, pays in ACP and receives a digital artifact with an execution and payment record. Creators can package expertise as reusable workflows and earn from paid runs.

## Initial customer and focus

The initial commercial focus is crypto project teams preparing launches, research and growth campaigns. ANCAP combines the offer, execution, settlement and receipt within one product. API and MCP integration surfaces support developer tools and AI agents.

Other projects form a staged expansion portfolio across business, commerce, life sciences, infrastructure and media. Research and partner briefs are distinguished from promises of physical, clinical or space services.

## Commercial model

The intended model combines ANCAP's own paid workflows with a platform fee on creator workflow runs. ACP is a settlement unit; token balances are neither company revenue nor a valuation. Creator and platform earnings depend on actual sales, execution costs and applicable offer terms.

## Evidence and current stage

The catalog, execution, settlement and receipt capabilities are implemented. Recorded local Ollama / qwen2.5:3b evidence shows a workflow executed without template fallback: price 14 test ACP, balance 50 → 36 ACP, receipt fee 1.4 ACP and captured payment status. This is a demonstration using test credits, not customer revenue or production-provider acceptance. A receipt does not certify the factual accuracy of an AI report.

The principal presentation surfaces are localized in five languages. Verified revenue and paying-customer metrics have not been supplied.

## Funding purpose

Funding is sought to complete the release, recruit a core team, obtain independent security review and validate paid demand through focused pilots. Pilot metrics will include paid runs, execution cost, refunds and repeat purchases.

A principal company in Cyprus and an additional German GmbH are planned. Registration, ownership, entity responsibilities and tax treatment remain subject to confirmation. The funding ask will follow a 12-month budget and vendor quotes; no assumed tax savings are included.
